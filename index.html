ALPINE DRONE LABINTERCEPTOR OPERATIONS

Choose how you want to fly.

MISSIONS · DEFEND

A scout crashing into the site fails the mission. You fly from a field pad at the site. ★ completed · ★★ no drone lost to a crash or empty battery · ★★★ every scout stopped more than 1 km from the site (the red dotted ring on the map).

MISSIONDEFEND

  • Scouts arrive one by one from the edge of the map, use valleys and low height to hide from your radar, and finally dive at the target — you can still stop them in the last seconds.
  • Identify contacts (radar, camera, thermal, gimbal), then pick a scout on the map (M) to pursue and ram it.
  • Each ram, crash or empty battery uses a drone; the next one waits on your field pad at the site. The raid keeps coming while you relaunch.
  • Your autopilot patrols around the defended site when it has nothing to investigate. Don't hit civil traffic.
