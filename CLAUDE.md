# DroneIntercept — Alpine Drone Lab

A single-file Three.js drone simulator game.

## Key facts
- **Main file:** `index.html` — all game code lives in one `<script type="module">` block inside it
- **Test tools:** `tools/` (see `tools/README.md`) — `check-syntax.js`, `smoke.js`, `bench.js` (pursuit benchmark, `--wind`, `--n`, `--kinds`, `--ref <git ref>` to compare with an older commit), `landing.js`, `traffic.js` (civil-traffic realism), `separation.js` (mid-air avoidance & collisions), `missions.js` (mission playtest with an auto-player), `shots.js` (model contact sheet), `bake-world.js` (fast-load site bake — see Fast loading), `npm test` = quick set. They build a test copy in `tools/.build/` (local three.js, seeded `Math.random` via `?seed=`, internals on `window.__t` — add names to `EXPOSE` in `tools/lib.js` when a test needs more). Note: three.js draws `Math.random` for every new object's UUID, so adding or removing meshes/materials at load shifts the seeded random sequence and the scenarios that follow — benchmark/mission results then change a little without any change to flying; compare means over many runs (`--n 30`, several seeds), not single runs. Run them after changing the game; run `bench.js` after touching guidance and `landing.js` after touching the landing.
- **Fast loading (on request):** the deterministic site search (`placeSites`, tens of millions of terrain samples — over half of the start-up script time) is baked into `index.html` as `SITE_BAKE` (between `/*BAKE*/ … /*/BAKE*/`, ~1.8 KB) with a fingerprint `SITE_KEY` = hash of the source of `placeSites`, `terrainNatural`, `fbm`, `vnoise`, `hash2`, `smoothstep`, `clamp`, `lerp` + `PEAKS`, `ARENA`, `WATER_Y`, `PAD_H`. Matching fingerprint → the baked layout is restored (identical map); otherwise the search runs as before. **After changing the terrain or the site placement run `node tools/bake-world.js`** (`smoke.js` warns 'bake is stale'). `vnoise` has `hash2` inlined (bit-identical). Load in the headless test browser 8.5-9.5 s → ~3.5-4 s; verified identical sites, reservoirs, roads (16 321 points), houses, streets and strike targets.
- **Renderer:** Three.js WebGL with ACES filmic tonemapping, instanced meshes for forests
- **Water rendering (on request — lakes shimmered even while hovering and sometimes 'vanished' for a frame):** the cause was the lake surface being ONE 14.6 km quad — depth interpolated across such huge triangles is imprecise (≈1 m errors at 100 m that jump with tiny camera moves; measured 7-10 % of pixels flipping water ⇄ ground for a 4 cm camera nudge). Now `PlaneGeometry(WORLD, WORLD, 96, 96)` (~150 m tiles) → 0.04-0.2 % (just edge changes). Also: the *rendered* lake bed drops 3 m within ±0.35 m of the waterline and the shore rises up to 0.6 m just above it (terrain mesh only — collisions/sites/map still use `terrainHeight`), the water has a small polygon offset and `renderOrder` −1 (fixed order among see-through things), camera near plane .5 (was .3). Check with a two-render pixel diff around lakes (camera nudged a few cm).
- **Physics:** 120 Hz fixed-step, interpolated rendering
- **Terrain:** FBM fractional Brownian motion via `terrainHeight(x, z)`
- **Arena:** `ARENA` = 6000 → playable square ±6 km (12 km; was 8.4 km — enlarged on request for realistic distances between places). Terrain mesh `SEG` 600 (~24 m cells). Trees, rocks and cloud banks scale with the area (`AREA_K`), so the density is unchanged. **Less water (on request):** `terrainNatural` lifts the low ground (`h += 16·(1 − smoothstep(0, 50, h))` before the lake floor) → ~15 % of the area is water instead of ~31 %; hills and mountains keep their height. Terrain mesh `WORLD` = 2·(ARENA+1300), mountain wall just outside ARENA, map `MAP_WORLD` = 2·ARENA+500 (the area boundary is no longer drawn on the map — on request; it still works in the game). Nothing may leave: drone/scouts/aircraft look ahead and turn back in time; if anything does cross it picks a random point well inside and returns with a brisker (but smooth) turn (`returning` flag / `state.borderPt`). Invisible backstop clamp at ±(ARENA+400) behind the mountains. Map estimates (`trackPos`) are dead-reckoned in 1 s steps with the same border logic: if the projected path would reach the border the estimate turns back towards a per-track random point inside, and never comes closer than 150 m to the border (also unknowns). Uncertainty circles are clipped to the area; labels flip left near the right border. Real aircraft start returning 250 m before the border. Light aircraft fly between destinations inside. **Exception — airliners are through-traffic** (user choice): ~400-460 km/h (112-128 m/s, low-altitude speed limit) across the area (`transit`) — not dead straight: every 30-75 s (first after 15-50 s) a new heading within ±35° of the original (`a.baseHdg`/`a.tgtHdg`, like ATC vectors), flown as a coordinated turn ≤ 25° bank, roll ~3°/s (≈3 km turn radius; such turns may run off the map, which is fine) — so they still cross and leave; spawned beyond an edge (`spawnTransitAirliner`), removed once they've left (`removeAmbient`); their tracks/estimates are hidden once outside. `randInside(m)` helper.

- **Autopilot in missions**: patrols a ring 0.9-2.4 km around the site (`pickSearchWaypoint`) at an economical ~50 m/s loiter (≈ 2× endurance; sandbox keeps 69 m/s); investigates by threat to the site from the radar track (close to the site and closing first — `scoreContact`), also contacts it has no line of sight to yet. **Cut-off**: against a target flying at the defended site (heading within ~37°), `attackGuidance` flies a collision course at full speed from anywhere in its front half, at any range (head-on law + PN finish), instead of a tail chase. The cut-off has hysteresis (on request — it kept dropping out against loitering raiders): it starts when the raider points within ~37° of the site (`run` > .8), stays on until it points > ~60° away (`run` < .5), the drone is really behind it, or the range has been opening for 1.5 s (`a.closingAt`; after a near miss the drone sat in front flying the same way and ran away from it for 30 s) — then no new cut-off for 6 s (`a.noCutUntil`) — not for a moment of not closing (`a.cut`; radio line at most every 20 s). **Mission tail chase is faster** (on request — it sat ~+10 m/s behind a raider and arrived too late): arrive ~600 m behind at +20 m/s (braking 3 m/s² instead of 1.5), then sprint 20 → 35 m/s once lined up on its tail (same `onTail` .55-.85 as the sandbox — sprinting sooner made it slide past a turning raider at max bank, ~43 m beside it); the sandbox keeps the gentle profile (350 m, +10 → 32 m/s). - **Collisions** (`hitsAirframe(o, boxes, pitch, yaw, roll)`): the drone's position in the object's local frame is tested against per-type boxes `[cx,cy,cz, hx,hy,hz]` grown by the drone's half-span `HIT_DR` = 0.45 m (rotor tips of the 1.2 m drone; was 1.5 m when the model was drawn 4× too big). Scouts: `SCOUT_SPEC[kind].hit` (light: body + wing; jet: body + wing; heavy: body + wing + tail); airliners: generated from the airframe (body, each wing panel, engines, fin, tailplane); light plane: `CESSNA_HIT`; bird flock: 10 m sphere.
- **Civil traffic attitude & climbs (realistic):** airliners and light aircraft bank into turns and pitch with climb/descent (`a.pitch`, ~2° nose-up cruise attitude; groups use rotation order YXZ = pitch, yaw, bank; also in replay and the hit test). Vertical speed is rate-limited in its own state `a.vy`: airliners ≤ 8 m/s, light aircraft +3.5/−4.5 m/s (6 m/s if within 50 m of the ground) holding height above the highest terrain ~1.2 km ahead, never below 30 m AGL. Light aircraft: 30° bank (~600 m turn radius), destinations anywhere ≥ 1.2 km away up to 900 m from the border (`randInside(900)` — note `randInside(m)` takes the *margin*), reached at 350 m, border look-ahead ≈ 1.7 turn radii — they roam most of the map. `tools/traffic.js`: airliners ≤ 25° bank, ≥ 2.8 km radius, ≤ 35° course change, pitch ≤ 6°, 40-125 s on the map; light aircraft 30° bank (40° in the rare border turn-back), pitch ≤ 5°, 22-29 of 36 map cells (6 × 6 grid over the area; 27-33 on the old 8.4 km map).
- **Mid-air separation & collisions (on request)** — `trafficSeparation(h)` in `updateWorld`, for scouts, airliners and light aircraft (not birds, not the drone): **avoidance** at 4 Hz — each aircraft reacts only to its single most urgent conflict (closest point of approach within its look-ahead, closer than both `airframeRadius` + a margin; `AVOID` = scout 12 s / 35 m, light aircraft 18 s / 60 m, airliner 28 s / 120 m) by turning away from the side the other will pass on (right if head-on) and climbing if it's the higher one / descending if lower (`o.avoid` {bank, vy, until}, used by `stepScout`/`stepPlane` instead of their own commands). One conflict at a time with limited bank/climb, deliberately: pairs pass safely but crowds can't be guaranteed to untangle (no 'avoid everyone' logic). **Collisions** every physics step: broadphase on bounding radii, then the real hit boxes as oriented boxes (separating-axis test `obbOverlap`), sub-stepped ≤ 25 cm relative motion between prev and current positions; 1.5 s spawn grace (`o.bornAt`). `midAirCollision(a, b)`: both explode with their momentum (airliner 6, light aircraft 2.6, heavy 2.4, other scouts 1.4), are removed (scouts `destroyed`, civil `gone`), a pursuit of either ends ('TARGET DESTROYED · mid-air collision') and the log says 'MID-AIR COLLISION · A × B · x km away'. `tools/separation.js`: 40/40 collision-course encounters (every type pair, head-on/crossing/overtake) avoided (closest 48-130 m between scouts, ~150 m with an airliner, 310-350 m airliner pair); two scouts head-on 60 m apart collide after 0.7 s; 95 scouts in one area: no NaNs/stuck circling (~5-6 % of the time avoiding), ~0.4 ms per physics step; 24 scouts converging on one point: 0/24 lost with avoidance, 2-4/24 without — real airframes are small, so even crowds rarely touch.
- Scouts look ≥ 20 s ahead for the border, ≈ 2.5 turn radii at their speed (~40 s for the jet, whose 30°-bank turn circle is ~1.5 km).

## Behaviour rules (agreed with the user)
- **Operator flags are final:** a flag can't be removed (on request) — pressing the same flag again does nothing (safe to spam while catching a far contact in the square; no log spam), the other flag switches THREAT ⇄ CIVIL ('Operator changed flag to …'; the debrief call follows the latest flag). A contact flagged ★ Threat / ★ Civil (`c.userMark`) is never inspected by the AI again — skipped as a candidate, added to `ignoredTargets`, and dropped at once if it was the current investigation (pursuit can still be ordered from the map).
- **Flight nudges (on request):** W/S = direct climb ~10 m/s / descent ~12 m/s while held (`G_.vsCmd`), on release that altitude is kept as `state.userAlt` for every search leg until the AI takes over (never below ~120 m AGL, max 1200 m AGL), A/D turn while held (`G_.nudgeRate`, full search bank); on release the search continues straight ahead from the new heading (new `searchWp` 1.5 km ahead, altitude kept). Only while the AI is just searching — not investigating, pursuing, on a manual waypoint or going home; then the keys are ignored and the log says why. When the AI picks up an unidentified contact it takes over (nudge state + altitude bias cleared). Flight state 'SEARCH · MANUAL NUDGE'. Keys cleared on window blur.
- **Classify loop:** drone investigates radar contacts (`c.detected`) not yet classified to ≥85 %; done when conf ≥ .85, a close look (< ½ classify range) or 45 s → `ignoredTargets`. No candidates → random search waypoints (`pickSearchWaypoint`).
- **Radar & tracks:** `radarRange()` 1.5 km clear / 1.2 mist / 0.9 rain = the green ring on the map, for **every kind of contact**, horizontal distance like the ring (`radarDetectRange`; on request — it used to be × radar cross-section `RCS`, so airliners were detected at 2.1 km outside the ring and prop scouts only at ~0.95 km; `RCS` is now unused), terrain-masked (`losPoint`). Check: farthest detections 1.53-1.58 km (a contact stays 'detected' until the next 1 s sweep). Identification is by camera/thermal, independent of the radar, so a tracked contact can still be identified just outside the ring (e.g. an airliner right after it left it, ~1.53 km) — that's the camera, not an error. One sweep per second (`RADAR_SWEEP`); plots are noisy (≈6 m + 1.5 % of range) — map/radar panel show the plot (extrapolated between sweeps), never the true position. Track `c.trk` {pos, vel, turn rate w, seen, lost}. Off radar: `trackPos()` dead-reckons along the measured turn (30 s) then straight; `trackSigma()` grows to ~1.5 km (rough guess after ~3-4 min). Track dropped when the drone gets within 100 m of the estimate and nothing is there, when the whole uncertainty area is swept and empty for 2 s, or after 10 min. The map never draws anything at a target's true position — only live radar plots or estimates. Pursuit of an undetected target flies to the estimate (phase 'search'); dropped track ends pursuit.
- **Identification** (UI always says *identified*, never *classified*): `classifyTick` (every 0.35 s, needs line of sight, independent of radar) — readings only while the object is unidentified, the active target (`state.aiTarget`), or seen up close (blur < .3, e.g. a fly-by); otherwise its % is frozen. While active/close, old votes decay (20 s → 3 s when right in front) so a good look corrects a wrong guess. Quality = sensorQuality(c) × (1 − blurAmount) — i.e. what the sensor actually resolves. camera best in clear daylight, ~15 % at night, poor in mist/rain; thermal ~78 % day or night, better in mist/rain, best on hot engines. HUD 'SENSOR … ID QUALITY x%' line under Flight State; confusions `CLS_CONFUSE`; votes → `c.guess` + `c.conf`; identified at ≥ 60 %; reclassification logged. Map/HUD/popup always show guess + %, never truth. Collision with airliner/birds = CIVILIAN INCIDENT (+ Mission Status counter). Big bottom-centre TERMINATE button during pursuit (key X).
- **Map:** radar coverage ring; home pad ⌂ with dashed line + 'HOME · x km' distance; compass rose **bottom-left** (moved on request — top-right it covered places; R 38, bold labels), placed inside the area boundary relative to `worldToMap(−ARENA, ARENA)` (north = map up = −Z, east = +X): ticks every 10°, bearing numbers 0, 30 … 330 outside the ring (0 red, 90/180/270 brighter), needle, the drone's heading marker + 'HDG xxx°'; live tracks solid, estimates (off radar) just the object faded at its estimated position with 'EST' + age — no uncertainty circle (removed on request; `trackSigma` still drives track dropping); **legend (update 3, on request):** `#mapLegend` beside the map — never over it: with the legend open `#mapWrap.leg` shrinks the map to leave room (below 560 px width it goes underneath, in 2 columns) — **shown by default on every screen size** (it used to start hidden below 1100 px, and that 'off' was saved), toggled by '◧ Legend' (localStorage `dl_legend`), symbols drawn as small SVGs in the map's own colours — drone, pad, radar range, waypoint · unknown, threat, civil, estimate, pursuit target · (missions only, `.mis`) defended site, ★★★ ring, site radar + MAST · roads, gravel tracks, radar station, FOB, dam, settlement names by size; annotation labels (RADAR / SITE RADAR rings) 7 px, site names by importance; unclassified = grey fuzzy blip (not selectable); classified = guess + % (threat guesses orange). Click empty map → manual waypoint (radar ignored until arrival). "✕ Waypoint" cancels and resumes search from the current position.
- **Ending a chase** (terminate, track lost) adds the target to `ignoredTargets` so the drone doesn't go back to it. Search waypoints inside the turn circle are re-picked (no orbiting).
- **Pursuit:** click an identified contact on the map → Yes/No popup (`#trackConfirm`, z-index above the map) → `pursuitMode` chase until collision. `endPursuit()` clears it. Any collision → `impact()`: both explode and vanish → slow-motion replay → `resetToPad('intercept')` (needs Launch again).
- **Takeoff:** `state.takeoffT`, ~8 s: vertical climb 0–2.5 s, pitch-over to cruise 2.5–7.5 s. Launch card hides on click.
- **Energy & stall:** `thrustAccel(V)` (falls with speed), climbs cost full g·sinγ, `inducedDecel(V,n)` in turns; throttle feeds these forward but is thrust-limited, so steep climbs / max-g turns bleed speed. `V_STALL` 31 m/s ×√n; below it `state.stalled` → wings level, nose −26°, recover above 1.3×V_STALL; HUD '⚠ STALL'. AI is energy-aware (bank limited so stall speed ≤ V/1.3, climb limited to spare thrust when slow); `V_MIN` 38 is the lowest commanded speed.
- **Flight model (active):** point-mass aircraft — airspeed `state.cruiseSpeed`, heading `rot.y`, bank `state.bank`, flight-path angle `state.gamma`; velocity always along the flight path, nose = gamma + AoA, roll = bank. Envelope consts `V_CRUISE` 69 m/s (~250 km/h, efficient search), `V_DASH` 111 (400 km/h, pursuit only), `V_MIN` 30, bank 65°/73°, climb/dive 22°/26° (pursuit ~31°/37°). Guidance fills `guide` (aim, alt, speed, bank, track/tau/ff for pursuit altitude).
- **Altitude planning:** one cruise altitude per leg from `routeFloor()` (max terrain on the straight route) → constant-slope climb/descent that arrives at the goal altitude; short lookahead `routePeak` only as safety.
- **Attack (`attackGuidance`):** rear-slot steering — aim at a point riding behind/beside the target on its *predicted turn arc* (`predictContact`/`solveRendezvous`), weight `wL` fades to 0 once committed behind (`commit`: behind AND same heading; inside 450 m rear half is enough) → collision course. Speed: far behind → catch up, arriving at the tail (~350 m) at +10 m/s; then, only once established on its tail (`onTail`), closure builds steadily from +10 to +32 m/s at impact (sure hit); turn-radius-fitted speed while converting. Altitude: copy target climb rate (`ff`) + null height error by arrival (`tau`), side offset (above 70% / below) collapses to 0 inside 40 m. HEAD-ON only when CPA geometry lines up.
